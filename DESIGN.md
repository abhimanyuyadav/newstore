# White storefront redesign

Design direction: a white, editorial fashion store with warm neutral image surfaces, thin dividers, large serif headlines, compact sans-serif navigation, and portrait photography. Existing catalog imagery and editable site copy remain the source of content.

Research reviewed:
- ARKET layout analysis: https://opendesign.cc/en/sites/arket — generous spacing, fine borders, restrained color and square controls.
- Fashion commerce reference: https://dribbble.com/shots/27637205-Fashion-E-Commerce-Website-Design-Clothing-Store-UI — white backgrounds, editorial photography, responsive product grids and clear option selection.

Applied to the home page, navigation, category tiles, shared product cards, collection browsing, footer, about page, and shopping/account surface styles. Home section enablement and ordering remain configurable. Collection links now filter the matching product flags.

Product cards lead to the product page to choose size and variants before adding to the bag. Checkout, WhatsApp support, wishlist, account, search and tracking remain accessible. Mobile navigation includes account and wishlist links; search is available at every width. Keyboard focus, reduced motion and image fallback states are included.

This redesign builds on the store reliability changes. Production server configuration and database migration requirements in README.md still apply; a visual redesign does not configure payment or storage services.
